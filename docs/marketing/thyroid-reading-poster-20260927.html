How to read it three steps

1

Find the cell

TSH runs across — the pituitary's signal. T4 runs down — what the thyroid makes. The cell where they meet is tagged THIS RESULT.

2

Check the bands

TSH, T4 and T3 each sit on their own scale: clear inside the reference band, hatched below or above it, a pointer at the value.

3

Read the pattern

The pattern is named and explained in two or three plain sentences — what it usually means and what is typically done next.
